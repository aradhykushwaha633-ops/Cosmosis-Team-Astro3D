Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

COSMOSIS is a free space-education website. This page explains what is collected, why, and how you stay in control. Questions: open an issue on the project page at github.com/aradhykushwaha633-ops/Cosmosis-Team-Astro3D.

What we collect

  • Account: name, email, role (student or teacher) and a one-way hash of your password. Guests give nothing.
  • Learning data: XP, quiz scores and Skill Lab progress are kept in your own browser.
  • Exams: a teacher's exam and a student's answers and marks, stored so the teacher can mark them.
  • Community posts: what you type into the community post form.

Cookies and Google Analytics

Google Analytics runs only if you press "Accept analytics" on the cookie banner. It counts visits and pages with an anonymised IP address. Choose "No thanks" and it never loads. Change your mind any time with the "Cookie settings" link in the footer. We also store small items in your browser (login session, language, sound and consent choice) that the site needs to work.

Services that receive data

  • Google Sheets / Apps Script: accounts, exam data and community posts.
  • Google Translate: page text is sent to Google when you pick another language.
  • Botpress: the chat assistant receives what you type to it.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile: may check that a form is filled by a person.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your data by email. Clearing your browser data removes everything stored on your device. We do not sell your data and do not show ads.

Students

Exam answers are visible only to the teacher who set the exam. Teachers should not ask students to enter more personal data than a name.
