From Solar Flare to Geomagnetic Storm

8 min
Flash of Light

A solar flare's X-rays and extreme UV light reach Earth at light speed, ionizing the upper atmosphere and blacking out HF shortwave radio on the sunlit side.

30m – hrs
Relativistic Particles

Accelerated protons arrive next. They can flip bits in satellite electronics and pose radiation risks for astronauts and polar flights.

1 – 3 days
The Plasma Cloud (CME)

A coronal mass ejection carries billions of tonnes of magnetized plasma. When it slams into Earth's magnetosphere, Kp spikes and auroras ignite.

Orbital Mechanics

When Can You Spot the ISS and Starlink?

Low-Earth orbit satellites do not carry headlights—you see them by reflected sunlight. The sweet spot is the 1–2 hours after sunset or before sunrise, when the ground is dark but the satellite 400–550 km overhead is still bathed in sunlight.

Your Optimal Satellite Spotting Windows Tonight

International Space Station

Orbiting at ~400 km altitude at 28,000 km/h (one lap every 92 minutes). It glides across the sky in 3 to 6 minutes as a brilliant, steady white light that never blinks and outshines Sirius.

Thermospheric Drag & Storms

Solar storms heat and expand Earth's upper atmosphere, drastically increasing aerodynamic drag on low satellites. In Feb 2022, a G2 storm caused 38 newly launched Starlinks to deorbit.
